Built to make business simpler.

Running a modern business means juggling a lot of moving parts. Bookings. Retention. Revenue. Engagement. The challenge isn't getting data — it's understanding it. fibodo Analytics brings everything together into one living snapshot of your performance, showing you exactly how your business is behaving in real time.

What you can do

Real-Time Insights

See bookings, payments, and engagement update live as your business moves.

Performance Trends

Spot peaks, dips, and opportunities over time — by service, client, or location.

Retention Metrics

Understand who stays, who drops off, and why — so you can take action early.

Marketing Impact

Track which campaigns drive the most conversions and revenue.

Customer Insights

Communication methods, Booking trends, Decision making.
