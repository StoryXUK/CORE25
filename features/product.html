Product Library

Create once. Sell everywhere.

The fibodo Product Library gives you one organised home for everything you sell — sessions, classes, courses, bundles, memberships, and vouchers. It's the control centre for your business, where every offer you create connects seamlessly with your storefront, your app, and your clients. THE OVERVIEW

Built to make business simpler.

When your services live across multiple platforms, consistency becomes chaos. The Product Library keeps your entire business aligned — every product, every price, every update reflected instantly across your ecosystem. Whether you're launching a single class or scaling to hundreds of offers, the Product Library makes it effortless to stay organised, accurate, and on-brand.

What you can do

1. Create Anything

Build sessions, classes, events, bundles, or memberships — all in one simple interface.

2. Add Rich Details

Descriptions, images, pricing, policies, and metadata for discoverability.

3. Publish Everywhere

Push products to your website, app, and partner sites with a single click.

4. Stay in Sync

Automatic integration with Bookings, CRM, Planner, and Payments — always accurate, never duplicated.

5. Update Instantly

Change a price, time, or description once — fibodo updates it everywhere, in real time.

6. Manage Effortlessly

Filter by category, tag, or revenue stream to track what's selling and what's next.
