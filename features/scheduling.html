THE OVERVIEW

Built to make business simpler.

You don’t have time to juggle diaries, reply to every message, and fix clashes by hand. That’s why fibodo’s Scheduling Module gives your clients a simple way to book — while applying your rules in the background. It’s your always-on booking desk — taking reservations, managing capacity, and updating your planner in real time, so your day runs smoothly from the first session to the last.

What you can do

Real-Time Availability

Show only the times you want to work and let clients book instantly into your live planner — no clashes, no confusion.

Smart Booking Rules

Control how and when people can book with buffers, cut-off times, and location-specific rules built in.

Automated Reminders

Reduce no-shows with automatic email and SMS reminders that keep clients on track.

Group & 1:1 Scheduling

Manage classes, courses, events, and 1:1 sessions from a single schedule — including recurring bookings and multi-week programmes.

Calendar & Team Management

See your whole team’s schedule at a glance, assign sessions, and balance workloads across venues and locations.

WHY IT MATTERS

More time for what matters.

  • Avoid double-bookings and last-minute chaos
  • Keep every session, coach, and venue in sync
  • Cut admin with self-serve booking and rescheduling
  • Make it easy for clients to book again and again
