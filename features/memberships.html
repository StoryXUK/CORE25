THE OVERVIEW

Built to make business simpler.

Memberships keep clients connected and committed. With fibodo, you can build subscription plans that match your business model — and deliver ongoing value that turns one-time customers into loyal members. Everything from payments to access rights is automated, giving your members a smooth, professional experience from day one.

What you can do

Create Flexible Membership Plans

Build simple or advanced memberships — monthly, annual, tiered, unlimited, credit based, or benefit-driven.

Automated Recurring Billing

Payments are charged automatically with retry logic, failed-payment handling, and renewal reminders built in.

Access & Entitlement Control

Automatically give members the right access — classes, content, events, passes, facilities, or priority booking windows.

Usage Tracking & Allowances

Track how many sessions, credits, or benefits each member has used — and automate renewals or top-ups.

Member Engagement Tools

Send updates, offers, and benefits directly through integrated CRM and marketing tools.
